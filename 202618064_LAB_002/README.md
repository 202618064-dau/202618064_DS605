## Key Observations
1. Females had a higher survival rate (~74%) than males (~19%).
2. 1st class passengers survived at a higher rate than 3rd class.
3. Fare and Pclass are negatively correlated — higher fare, better class.
4. Passengers traveling alone had lower survival rate than families of 2-4.
5. Fare has strong right-skew with several high-value outliers.
6. Age has ~20% missing values, imputed using mean/median.
7. Higher fare passengers cluster toward survival in the scatter plot.
